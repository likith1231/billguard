Sum insured: Rs 5,00,000          Insured: Ananya Rao, age at entry 31
Add-ons: Protect Plus rider

Clause 4.1 — Room rent: Room rent up to 1% of sum insured per day (Rs 5,000).
Clause 4.2 — Proportionate deduction: If the insured opts for a room above the
eligible category, associated medical expenses shall be payable in the proportion
of eligible room rent to actual room rent, except for ICU charges, pharmacy,
consumables, implants, medical devices and diagnostics.
Clause 5.3 — Co-payment: A co-payment of 10% shall apply on admissible claims
where the insured person is aged 61 years or above at entry.
Section 7.2 — Protect Plus rider: Covers the items listed in Annexure I (list of
non-payable items) of the IRDAI guidelines, including gloves and admission kits.
